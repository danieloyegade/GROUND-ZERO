# Design Document

## Site Concept
GROUND ZERO is a minimal, editorial, image-led site with a restrained premium tone. The experience should feel deliberate and spacious rather than decorative.

## Entry Sequence
- A full-frame intro image opens the experience.
- Descending floor labels frame the transition:
  - EIGHTH FLOOR
  - FOURTH FLOOR
  - FIRST FLOOR
  - GROUND FLOOR
  - GROUND ZERO
- The transition resolves into a swipe-up reveal into the homepage.

## Navigation
- Fixed navigation remains visible once the homepage is revealed.
- `GROUND ZERO` sits on the left.
- `INFORMATION` sits on the right.

## Page Behavior
- The homepage uses a freeform editorial project layout.
- Project titles appear at the bottom-left on hover.
- Hover treatment should be restrained, using darkening rather than dramatic motion or effects.

## Typography
- Primary: Satoshi
- Secondary: Cormorant Garamond
- Use Cormorant Garamond sparingly for emphasis rather than as the dominant face.

## Experience Constraints
- Keep motion subtle and purposeful.
- Prioritize image hierarchy, spacing, and typography over interface chrome.
- Avoid flashy interactions that weaken the editorial tone.
